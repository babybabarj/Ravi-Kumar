Effective date: 1 August 2026 Last updated: 1 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ravi Kumar, operating personally from Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India, collects, uses, shares, stores and protects personal information through RaviKumarAI.com and related service communications.

In this policy, “I”, “me” and “my” refer to Ravi Kumar. “Website” refers to RaviKumarAI.com. “You” refers to a visitor, enquirer, prospective client, client, partner or other person whose personal information is processed.

This policy is intended to reflect applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, as their relevant provisions come into force. India's framework has phased commencement: selected institutional and procedural provisions commenced in November 2025, while further provisions are scheduled for later commencement. This policy does not claim that every operational provision was already in force on 1 August 2026. Other rights may apply depending on your location and the circumstances.
