About Me

Connected systems,
not scattered tools.

I help businesses turn fragmented, repetitive and manually managed operations into connected, AI-enabled systems that owners can actually see and control.

My work goes beyond building an individual website or chatbot.

I design practical digital solutions that bring administration, finance, marketing, customer management, content, reporting and daily operations into one structured environment. That gives business owners better visibility, reduces repetitive work and helps teams operate more efficiently.

My approach begins with understanding how the organisation currently works. I identify repetitive tasks, disconnected systems, reporting gaps and operational bottlenecks — then help structure and implement the right combination of dashboards, workflows, AI assistants and automation.

I work across leading AI platforms, using prompt-led, AI-assisted and no-code or low-code development to turn business requirements into functional digital products. That covers AI agents, multi-step workflows, human approval systems and multimodal AI across text, images, audio and video.
