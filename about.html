Principles

Technology should earn its place in the workflow.

01
Business firstStart with the problem and desired outcome, not the newest tool.
02
Clarity before complexityA simpler system people actually use is better than an impressive system nobody understands.
03
Human responsibilityAI can support repetitive work and decision-making, but important accountability should remain clear.
04
Smallest useful solutionBuild enough to prove the workflow before adding unnecessary complexity.
05
Verify before claimingTest what can be tested and keep assumptions separate from facts.
Approach

Understand first. Automate second.

Automating a broken process just gets you the wrong answer faster.

01

Understand

Map how the work really moves today, including manual handoffs and workarounds.

02

Identify

Find repetitive tasks, disconnected information, reporting gaps and bottlenecks.

03

Structure

Decide what needs a workflow, a dashboard, an application, an AI assistant, or no new technology at all.

04

Implement

Build, test, document and hand over the agreed solution with human approval where it matters.
