Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026 Last updated: 1 August 2026

This policy provides general principles for professional-service engagements with Ravi Kumar. It does not apply to the purchase of products because RaviKumarAI.com does not sell products through the Website.

A signed proposal, statement of work, invoice or other project agreement controls where it contains specific cancellation or refund terms.

1. Before work begins

A client may request cancellation before work begins.

Any refund will depend on:

  • Whether discovery or planning has already been completed
  • Whether time has been reserved
  • Whether third-party services have been purchased
  • Whether custom research or preparation has started
  • Payment-provider or transfer charges
  • The accepted proposal or invoice terms

Where no work or committed cost exists, a refund may be considered after deducting non-recoverable fees.

2. Initial payments and deposits

An initial payment may reserve capacity and authorise work to begin.

Once work has started, the initial payment is generally applied to completed work, planning, allocated time and committed costs. It is not automatically refundable merely because the client changes direction, delays inputs or decides not to continue.

Any non-refundable amount must be stated clearly in the project documents.

3. Milestone payments

Amounts paid for completed and accepted milestones are generally not refundable.

If a project ends during a milestone, the client may be charged for:

  • Work completed
  • Approved work in progress
  • Time reasonably committed
  • Third-party costs
  • Handover preparation
  • Contractual cancellation charges, if any

Any balance eligible for refund will be calculated after these amounts.

4. Client delays

A project may be paused when required information, feedback, content, access, approvals or payments are delayed.

A prolonged delay may require:

  • Rescheduling
  • A revised timeline
  • A restart fee
  • Closure of the project
  • Re-scoping because technology or requirements changed

Payments are not automatically refundable because of client-caused delay.

5. Change of scope

A request outside the accepted scope is a change request and may affect cost and timeline.

Declining to fund an additional request does not create a refund right for correctly completed original-scope work.

6. Third-party costs

The following are normally non-refundable once purchased or committed:

  • Domain registration
  • Hosting
  • Advertising spend
  • API or AI usage
  • Messaging and telephone fees
  • Email services
  • Software subscriptions
  • Stock assets
  • External specialists
  • App-store or platform fees
  • Payment-processing charges

Third-party refund rules also apply.

7. Digital deliverables

Refunds are not normally available for completed and delivered digital work merely because:

  • The client changes preference
  • A platform changes policy
  • A third-party service becomes unavailable
  • Expected business results are not achieved
  • Search or advertising performance differs from expectations

This does not affect rights relating to material failure to deliver the agreed scope or rights that cannot legally be excluded.

8. Termination by Ravi Kumar

I may suspend or terminate work for:

  • Non-payment
  • Unlawful or harmful use
  • Misrepresentation
  • Abuse or harassment
  • Repeated failure to provide required inputs
  • Security risk
  • Material breach
  • Conflict or legal risk
  • Work becoming technically or legally unsuitable

The financial position will be assessed based on work completed, commitments, project terms and applicable law.

9. Quality concerns

A client should report a claimed scope defect promptly and provide enough detail to assess it.

Where the project agreement includes a correction or support period, reasonable scope-related defects should normally be addressed through that process before a refund is considered.

A change in taste or a new request is not necessarily a defect.

10. Refund request process

Send a written request to work.ravijethani@gmail.com with:

  • Client name
  • Project name
  • Invoice or payment reference
  • Amount
  • Cancellation date
  • Reason
  • Relevant project clause
  • Requested resolution

I will review the accepted terms, work records, deliverables and committed costs.

11. Processing

Approved refunds will be sent through a reasonable available method. Banking, currency-conversion and payment-provider processing times may apply.

12. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy removes a mandatory right available under applicable law.
