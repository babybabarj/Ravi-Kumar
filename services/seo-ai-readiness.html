AI readiness

AI readiness does not require hidden AI keywords. It benefits from many of the same foundations as modern SEO:

  • Clear factual descriptions
  • Consistent names, services, locations and contact details
  • Accessible HTML
  • Descriptive headings
  • Concise definitions
  • Crawlable links
  • Structured data
  • Public policies and trust information
  • llms.txt as an optional curated summary
  • Explicit crawler choices in robots.txt
  • Current, verifiable project and profile information

No one can guarantee that a specific AI system will cite or recommend a website.

Content architecture

A small professional website should not create dozens of near-identical pages. Each indexable URL should answer a distinct user need.

For RaviKumarAI.com, the recommended structure separates:

  • Personal profile
  • Service overview
  • Specific service categories
  • Agency partnership
  • Projects
  • Process
  • Frequently asked questions
  • Contact
  • Trust and legal information

Country or city pages should be created only when there is enough unique local context, proof and intent to justify them.

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee a number-one ranking?

No. Search rankings are determined by search engines and influenced by competition, authority, relevance, technical quality and many external factors.

Is llms.txt required?

No. It is an emerging convention, not a universal standard or ranking guarantee. It can provide a concise, LLM-friendly summary alongside normal SEO foundations.

Should every service and country have a separate page?

Only when each page has distinct, useful and substantial content. Cloned pages can create a weak user experience and may resemble doorway content.

Does structured data guarantee rich results?

No. Valid structured data can help search systems understand a page, but display decisions remain with the search engine.

Can you fix PageSpeed issues?

Performance work can address images, scripts, CSS, fonts, caching, rendering and DOM size. Scores may still vary between tests.
