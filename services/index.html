What every engagement should define

Before work begins, the proposal or statement of work should identify:

  • Objective
  • Scope and exclusions
  • Deliverables
  • Client responsibilities
  • Required access and content
  • Timeline and milestones
  • Review and revision process
  • Fees and payment schedule
  • Third-party costs
  • Intellectual-property treatment
  • Confidentiality requirements
  • Support or maintenance terms

What I do not promise

I do not guarantee search rankings, advertising performance, a specific revenue result, uninterrupted third-party platforms or error-free AI output. Digital work can improve the quality of execution and increase the probability of better outcomes, but results also depend on the offer, market, budget, content, sales process, platform rules and client participation.
