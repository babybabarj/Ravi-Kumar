Real Estate AI

AI automation for real estate lead conversion.

Most property teams do not lose deals because they lack enquiries. They lose deals because the response is slow, the lead is not qualified, the right agent is not assigned and follow-up depends on memory.

A practical real estate AI system should ask for budget, location, timeline and buying intent, route hot enquiries to the right agent, help book viewings and keep managers aware of lead status. This is the kind of automation Ravi Kumar builds for real estate teams across the UAE, UK, USA and India.

  • Use WhatsApp as the first response channel when buyers already enquire there.
  • Connect website, ad and portal enquiries into one lead view.
  • Track viewings, follow-ups, agent performance and project interest in one dashboard.
