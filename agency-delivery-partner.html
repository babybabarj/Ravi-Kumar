Partner Support

Confidential delivery support for agencies and consultants

Your brand. My execution.

I support agencies, consultants and business-service firms that need additional delivery capacity without immediately building a larger in-house team.

Partner-safe working principles

Confidentiality

Project information, client details, credentials and commercial terms should be used only for the agreed purpose.

NDA readiness

A mutual or partner-provided NDA can be reviewed before sensitive information is shared.

Clear ownership

The statement of work should identify whether deliverables are partner-branded, neutral or credited.

No client solicitation

I will not knowingly use a partner engagement to directly solicit the partner's client outside the agreed working relationship.

Controlled communication

Direct client communication occurs only when the partner approves it and defines the expected role.

Transparent dependencies

Third-party subscriptions, external specialists, platform fees and client-supplied requirements should be identified clearly.
