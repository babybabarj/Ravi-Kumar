Accessibility Statement

Effective date: 1 August 2026 Last updated: 1 August 2026

Ravi Kumar is committed to making RaviKumarAI.com usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people who use keyboards, screen readers, zoom, voice input or other assistive technologies.

Accessibility target

The Website aims to follow recognised accessibility practices and work towards alignment with WCAG 2.2 Level AA where reasonably achievable.

This is an ongoing effort and not a claim that every page, external link or third-party tool is fully conformant.

Measures

The Website should aim to provide:

  • Semantic headings
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation
  • Visible focus states
  • Sufficient contrast
  • Meaningful link text
  • Text alternatives for informative images
  • Empty alternative text for decorative images
  • Labelled form controls
  • Clear validation messages
  • Responsive layouts
  • Resizable text
  • Reduced-motion support
  • Touch targets with suitable size and spacing
  • Captions or text alternatives for important media where practical

Third-party content

External portfolio websites, embedded services, communication tools and third-party platforms may have their own accessibility limitations. I do not control every third-party experience but welcome reports about barriers encountered through a feature linked or embedded on this Website.

Known limitations

Temporary limitations may arise from:

  • Older project screenshots
  • Experimental demonstrations
  • Third-party widgets
  • Embedded media
  • PDF or downloadable content
  • Rapidly changing prototypes

Where a reasonable alternative format is available, it can be requested.

Feedback

Report an accessibility issue by emailing work.ravijethani@gmail.com with:

  • Page URL
  • Description of the problem
  • Device and browser
  • Assistive technology, if relevant
  • Preferred way to receive the information

I will make a reasonable effort to understand and address the issue.
