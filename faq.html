Project fit

What information should I send first?

Explain the business objective, current situation, main problem, target users, expected outcome and preferred timeline. Include the current website or workflow where relevant.

Do you work with existing websites and systems?

Yes. Work can focus on audit, redesign, repair, performance, SEO, automation, new features or integration.

Can you build a demonstration before a full project?

A demonstration may be proposed when it is useful and proportionate. Its fee, ownership and reuse terms should be agreed before work begins.

Can you join an existing team?

Yes, subject to a defined role, access, communication process and responsibility boundaries.

Pricing and payment

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on scope, complexity, timeline, integrations, content readiness, support requirements and third-party costs. A written proposal should define the commercial terms.

Are third-party costs included?

Not unless expressly stated. Hosting, domains, advertising spend, APIs, AI usage, messaging, email, CRM, stock assets and external subscriptions are usually separate.

Do you require an advance?

Many projects require an initial payment before work begins. The exact schedule should be stated in the proposal or invoice.

Are payments refundable?

Refund eligibility depends on the signed project terms, work completed, costs committed and cancellation timing. Read the Cancellation & Refund Policy and the project-specific agreement.

AI and automation

Can AI output be guaranteed to be accurate?

No. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Important outputs require source control, testing, human review and escalation.

Can you connect an AI workflow to WhatsApp or a CRM?

Potentially, when approved APIs and integrations are available. Provider policies, fees, verification and technical limits apply.

Will automation remove the need for people?

Not necessarily. The goal is usually to reduce repetitive work and improve response, routing and consistency while retaining people for judgment and exceptions.

Websites, SEO and advertising

Can you guarantee a 100/100 PageSpeed score?

No. The implementation can be optimised, but scores vary by test environment, hosting, third-party scripts, assets and ongoing changes.

Can you guarantee Google rankings?

No. SEO improves technical and content foundations, but rankings remain controlled by search engines and depend on competition, authority, relevance and external factors.

Can you guarantee leads from ads?

No. Advertising results depend on the offer, market, budget, creative, landing page, tracking, response time and sales process.

Is media spend included in ads support?

Normally no. Advertising spend is paid separately to the platform unless the proposal expressly states otherwise.

Agency partnerships

Can you work confidentially for an agency?

Yes. I can work behind the scenes under an agreed NDA and partner-safe delivery arrangement.

Will you contact the agency's client?

Only if authorised and under the agreed communication model.

Can the work be delivered under our brand?

That can be arranged when the proposal defines branding, attribution, handover and portfolio-use rights.

Support and ownership

Who owns the final work?

Ownership depends on the project agreement, full payment, third-party licences and any reusable components. The proposal should state the applicable terms.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes, through a separate support scope where required.
