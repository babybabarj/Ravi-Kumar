Cookie Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026 Last updated: 1 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how RaviKumarAI.com may use cookies and similar technologies.

The Website is operated personally by Ravi Kumar in Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a browser or device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels, tags and software development kit identifiers.

They can support security, remember preferences, measure usage and help understand whether a campaign or page is effective.

2. Categories

Strictly necessary

These support core functions such as security, network delivery, consent storage, form protection and session operation.

They may be used without an optional consent choice where permitted because the requested service cannot work properly without them.

Analytics

These help understand page visits, performance, navigation and errors.

Analytics should be configured to minimise unnecessary data and should load only after the required consent where applicable.

Functionality

These remember optional choices or enable enhanced features.

Marketing

These may measure advertising, build audiences or connect visits with campaign platforms.

Marketing technologies should remain disabled until the required choice has been made.

3. Current implementation rule

The actual cookie banner and policy must match the tools deployed on the live Website.

Before publication, create a live cookie inventory showing:

  • Cookie or storage name
  • Provider
  • Purpose
  • Category
  • Expiry
  • First-party or third-party status
  • Whether it loads before consent

Do not list a tool that is not installed. Do not omit a tool that is installed.

4. Typical tools that may be used

Depending on the Website configuration, categories may include:

  • Hosting and content-delivery security
  • Form-spam protection
  • Consent-preference storage
  • Google Analytics or a similar analytics service
  • Google Ads conversion measurement
  • Meta campaign measurement
  • Embedded video or scheduling tools
  • Error monitoring

Each third party processes information under its own policies.

5. Consent

Where consent is required, the Website should:

  • Present Accept, Reject and Manage choices
  • Keep non-essential tools disabled before a choice
  • Avoid pre-ticked optional categories
  • Make withdrawal as easy as acceptance
  • Remember the choice for a reasonable period
  • Provide a persistent Cookie Settings link
  • Record the consent event where required

Rejecting optional cookies should not block access to normal Website content.

6. Managing cookies

You can manage cookies through:

  • The Website's Cookie Settings control
  • Browser settings
  • Device settings
  • Provider opt-out tools where available

Blocking some technologies may affect optional features.

7. Browser signals

The Website should not claim to honour Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control unless that behaviour has been technically implemented and tested.

8. Retention

Cookie duration varies by purpose. Essential preferences may remain until expiry or manual deletion. Analytics and marketing retention should be limited to the configured business need and provider settings.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Website changes tools or practices. The live cookie inventory and consent configuration should be reviewed whenever a new third-party script is added.
