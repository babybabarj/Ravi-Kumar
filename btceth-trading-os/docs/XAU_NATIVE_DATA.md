# XAUUSDT native data admission

Status: **RAW AUDIT ONLY — RESEARCH ADMISSION BLOCKED** (2026-09-23).

Binance's [launch notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/ecf7318c0d434c339e80878588e700d0) says the TradFi XAUUSDT perpetual became available to all eligible markets on 2026-01-05, without a UTC time. Official, checksum-verified XAUUSDT futures klines, trades, and funding files begin on 2025-12-11. Their existence does not establish who could trade them. Rows before **2026-01-06 00:00 UTC** are quarantined from executable research. This date is a conservative admission floor, not a claim about the first trade or every account's eligibility.

The contract and its underlying commodity use different clocks. Binance says the [perpetual trades 24/7](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/ecf7318c0d434c339e80878588e700d0) while the underlying has market hours; its [September session notice](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/f69c05ebdba7422e9c614685abe84dc5) says the change does not interrupt contract operations. An underlying weekend or holiday therefore does not automatically excuse a missing XAUUSDT contract bar. The [tradingSchedule API](https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-derivatives-trading-usd-s-m-futures/api/rest-api/market-data) describes underlying asset sessions, not a verified historical contract outage calendar.

`tools/audit_xau_native_bars.py` downloads official archives with `.CHECKSUM` verification, preserves RAW receipts, validates every bar and funding row, and reports row counts, timestamps, gaps, duplicates, ordering, OHLC, nonpositive price where relevant, negative volume, physical SHA, and logical SHA. Premium/index values may be zero or negative. In June 2026, the monthly mark, index, and premium files omit 2026-06-29; the checksum-verified daily files each supply the missing 1,440 bars. The audit reports both the original source defect and the repair source, without manufacturing bars. Its machine-readable output is `reports/XAU_NATIVE_COVERAGE_AUDIT.json`.

Binance's [public archive documentation](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data) says daily files appear the next day and monthly files on the first Monday; published files can later change. The existing downloader detects checksum changes and writes `.part` files before promoting RAW bytes. The XAU catalog is separate at `config/xau_datasets.yaml` to preserve the original BTC/ETH historical verification scope.

Historical rule replay remains blocked. Official notices identify [2026-01-23 funding interest](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/4636922abdba4e5787c5f7643f8170d7), [2026-01-29 index constituent weights](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/announcement/detail/9c9e956d5dae412fadcd62c88b3d7849), [2026-01-30 funding changes](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/db04567123a44a89b112b9ae403a9c7d), [2026-05-08 off-hours index mode](https://www.binance.com/en/square/post/319824740830977), and the [2026-09-15 underlying session change](https://www.binance.com/en/support/announcement/detail/f69c05ebdba7422e9c614685abe84dc5). The current `exchangeInfo` snapshot is not proof of historical tick size, step size, margin, contract size, leverage, or account eligibility. Historical best bid/ask and executable depth have not been established by this audit. Until those gates are resolved, no XAU strategy may be promoted and no historical fill quality may be claimed from these bars alone.
