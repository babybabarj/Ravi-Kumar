Important Updates on Trading Hours and Price Index Calculation Mode of Commodity-Based TradFi Perpetual Contracts (2026-09-15)

Published on 2026-09-15 02:15
Updated on 2026-09-15 06:05

This is a general Binance Exchange Notice. Products and services referred to here may not be available in your region.

Fellow Binancians,

Binance constantly reviews its product and service offerings to ensure the best user experience for all users. Effective from 2026-09-15 21:00 (UTC), the trading hours and price index calculation mode for each trading session of commodity-based TradFi perpetual contracts are as follows:

Trading Sessions

Price Index Calculation Mode

Regular Trading Hours

Note: From every Sunday 18:00 (ET) to every Friday 17:00 (ET)

Standard mode: The price index is updated every second as a weighted average of all constituents where the Price Index is constructed from constituent prices quoted by third-party data vendors. 

Weekends and public holidays

Note: The weekends trading hours start from every Friday 17:00 (ET) to every Sunday 18:00 (ET).

Orderbook EWMA mode

Notes: 

  • The change in the trading hours will not impact commodity-based TradFi perpetual contracts trading operations.

  • The new trading hours will be applied on all commodity-based TradFi perpetual contracts, such as XAUUSDT, XAGUSDT, XPTUSDT, XPDUSDT, COPPERUSDT, CLUSDT, BZUSDT, NATGASUSDT, and any new commodity-based TradFi perpetual contracts to be listed on Binance Futures in the future. 

  • This information is released as a Notice under Binance Clearing Procedures. 

  • In case there is a discrepancy between this notice and any Futures FAQs, please refer to this notice for the most accurate and updated information.

  • There may be discrepancies between this original content in English and any translated versions. Please refer to the original English version for the most accurate information, in case any discrepancies arise.
