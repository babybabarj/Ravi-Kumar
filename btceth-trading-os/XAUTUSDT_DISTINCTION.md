# XAUTUSDT remains separate

XAUTUSDT refers to a perpetual associated with Tether Gold (XAUT), a tokenized gold asset. XAUUSDT is Binance's TradFi gold price perpetual. They have different underlying exposures, listing histories, liquidity, funding, and tracking risks.

The current Trading OS does not enable XAUTUSDT. `resolve_instrument("XAUTUSDT")` reports that support is unavailable. `XAEUSDT` is invalid and is never silently mapped to either product. XAUT support requires its own official contract verification, instrument specification, data partitions, rule history, session treatment, research, and risk acceptance.
